About SafeSip

According to research conducted by our team, 11.1% of students have been spiked themselves and 61.1% of them know someone who has been spiked.

There are many factors that can increase your chances of being spiked. The two most common reasons are: leaving a drink unattended to dance or through someone slipping something into a drink without you noticing.

SafeSip aims to eliminate these two risk factors through design. The closed top not only makes it harder to breach, but also provides a non-spill lid that eliminates the need to put your drink down when wanting to dance. This is further helped by the strap which allows you to always have your drink on you.
